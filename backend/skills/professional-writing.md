# professional-writing

Use when producing any written document: email, report, article, proposal, memo, or announcement.

## Process

1. **Know the audience and purpose.** Ask or infer: who reads this, what do they need to decide or do, what tone fits (formal, friendly, persuasive, neutral)?
2. **Structure before writing.** Outline: opening (context + purpose), body (key points + evidence), closing (next steps / ask). Use short paragraphs and headings for anything longer than a page.
3. **Lead with the conclusion.** Put the main point or recommendation in the first paragraph; details follow. Busy readers should get the answer without scrolling.
4. **Be concise and specific.** Cut filler words. Prefer concrete numbers and examples over vague claims.
5. **Use active voice** unless a passive construction is genuinely clearer.
6. **Proofread for tone and errors.** Read aloud once; check spelling, grammar, and that the tone matches the audience.
7. **Format for skimming.** Bullet lists for 3+ items, bold for key terms, one idea per paragraph.

## Email-specific

- Clear, specific subject line (not "Hi" or "Quick question").
- Greeting, then 2-4 short paragraphs max.
- Explicit call to action or question at the end.
- Close with a polite sign-off matched to the relationship.
