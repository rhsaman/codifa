# seo-optimization

When optimizing a page or site for search engines:

1. **Keyword research first** — identify the primary keyword (high intent, realistic volume) and 3-5 secondary keywords. Use them naturally; never stuff.
2. **Title tag** — under 60 chars, primary keyword near the front, unique per page, compelling for clicks.
3. **Meta description** — 140-160 chars, summarize value, include a call to action, unique per page.
4. **URL structure** — short, lowercase, hyphen-separated, keyword-relevant, no stop words or params when avoidable.
5. **Headings hierarchy** — one H1 per page with the primary keyword; logical H2/H3 structure that mirrors the content outline.
6. **Content quality** — answer the searcher's intent fully; use secondary keywords in subheadings and body; add internal links to related pages and external links to authoritative sources.
7. **Structured data** — add JSON-LD (Article, Product, FAQ, BreadcrumbList as applicable) and validate with the Rich Results Test.
8. **Technical checks** — canonical tag, robots meta, sitemap entry, fast load time, mobile-friendly viewport, descriptive alt text on images.
9. **Measure** — after publishing, check impressions/clicks in Search Console and iterate on the pages with impressions but low CTR.
